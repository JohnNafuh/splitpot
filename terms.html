Terms & responsible play

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: you must be 18 or older. Results are decided by honest votes. If votes don't match, an admin decides, and if nobody can tell who won, everyone is refunded minus the platform fee.

1. Who can play

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • One account per person. Give accurate details.
  • We may ask you to verify your identity at any time.

2. Test money

While splitpot is in testing, every balance is test money with no cash value. It can't be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.

3. How rooms work

  • A host creates a room and sets the game, the stake and the number of players.
  • Your stake is locked when you create or join a room. All stakes together form the pot.
  • You play the game outside splitpot, then every player votes for the winner.
  • The winner receives the pot minus the platform fee.

4. Platform fee

splitpot keeps 15% of pots under ₦5,000 and 10% of pots of ₦5,000 and above. The fee is taken from the pot before the winner is paid.

5. Results and disputes

  • Your vote is final once submitted.
  • If all votes match, the winner is paid straight away. No proof is needed.
  • If votes differ, the room goes under review and the stakes stay locked. Each player is asked to upload a screenshot or screen recording of the result.
  • An admin reviews the proof and picks the winner. The admin's decision is final.
  • If the admin can't tell who won, every player is refunded minus the platform fee, shared equally.
  • If not everyone votes within 30 minutes of the first vote, the player with the most votes wins. A tie goes under review.

6. Refunds without a fee

  • A room that doesn't fill within 30 minutes, or a game that isn't finished within 30 minutes, is cancelled and every stake is refunded in full.
  • You can leave a room before it fills and get your full stake back. If the host leaves, the room is cancelled and everyone is refunded in full.

7. Fair play

Do not vote falsely, upload fake or edited proof, use more than one account, or work with another player to cheat. We may suspend accounts and hold balances while we investigate.

8. Responsible play

  • Only play with money you can afford to lose.
  • Never chase losses and never borrow to play.
  • Take regular breaks. If playing stops being fun or starts to cause problems, stop and talk to someone you trust.

9. Your information

We collect your email, username, date of birth, game history and any proof you upload. We use it to run splitpot, check your age and prevent fraud. Proof files can only be seen by the players in that room and by admins. We don't sell your information.

10. Changes

We may update these Terms. If you keep using splitpot after a change, you accept the new Terms.
